About

I lead product for SuperInvesting, an AI research platform covering 5,000+ Indian stocks. I work with a team of 8 across engineering, design, QA and analytics, and own the weekly business review, AI cost, and the support experience.

I came to product from the engineering side of the same platform. As an AI engineer I evaluated agents, fixed prompts and co-authored a benchmark paper comparing SuperInvesting with GPT, Gemini, Claude and Perplexity. It means I know how the AI behaves with real users, not just in demos.
